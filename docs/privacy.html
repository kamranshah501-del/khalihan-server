Khalihan — Privacy Policy

Effective: 7 October 2026 · App: Khalihan (in.khalihan.app)

सरल शब्दों में: Khalihan कोई खाता या मोबाइल नंबर नहीं माँगता, विज्ञापन नहीं दिखाता और आपका डेटा बेचता नहीं। आपका नाम, फसलें और लक्ष्य-भाव आपके फ़ोन में ही रहते हैं। लोकेशन सिर्फ़ ज़िला पहचानने और मौसम दिखाने के लिए, और सिर्फ़ आपकी अनुमति से। "सुझाव दें" में आप जो लिखकर भेजते हैं, वह हम तक पहुँचता है — उसमें अपना फ़ोन नंबर या निजी जानकारी न लिखें।

सोप्या शब्दांत: Khalihan खाते किंवा मोबाईल नंबर मागत नाही, जाहिराती दाखवत नाही आणि तुमचा डेटा विकत नाही. तुमचे नाव, पिके आणि लक्ष्य-भाव तुमच्या फोनमध्येच राहतात. लोकेशन फक्त जिल्हा ओळखण्यासाठी आणि हवामानासाठी, आणि फक्त तुमच्या परवानगीने. "सूचना द्या" मध्ये तुम्ही जे लिहून पाठवता ते आमच्यापर्यंत पोहोचते — त्यात तुमचा फोन नंबर किंवा खाजगी माहिती लिहू नका.

1. What Khalihan does

Khalihan shows farmers mandi (market) prices, weather and price alerts. It has no user accounts, no login and no advertising.

2. Information that stays on your phone

Your language, state and district, the name you type (optional), the crops you track, your price targets and alert settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your information, show ads, collect your phone number, contacts, photos or files, or track your location in the background.

5. Services we rely on

Google / Firebase privacy policy · Open-Meteo terms · BigDataCloud privacy policy

6. Where the prices come from

Prices come from Agmarknet (via data.gov.in, Government of India) and the Maharashtra State Agricultural Marketing Board (MSAMB). They are shown for information only and can be 1–2 days old; always confirm at the mandi before selling. Khalihan is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any government body.

7. Your choices

You can turn off location or notifications at any time in your phone's settings; the app keeps working with the district you chose.

Khalihan has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign out of or close. To remove your data:

  1. Data on your phone (name, crops, price targets, settings): uninstall Khalihan, or open Android Settings → Apps → Khalihan → Storage → Clear data. This removes it at once and also resets the app's random IDs.
  2. Suggestions you sent: email kamranshah501@gmail.com with the subject "Khalihan – Delete my data", and tell us roughly what you wrote, when, and your district so we can find it. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.
  3. Usage and crash data: email us the same way and we will ask Google Firebase to delete what is linked to your phone where we can identify it. In any case this data is not linked to your name or phone number and is deleted automatically: crash reports after 90 days, usage data after 14 months at most.

What we keep: daily market price averages (they contain nothing about you) and anonymous totals such as the number of farmers using a feature.
