"सूचना द्या" मध्ये तुम्ही जे लिहून पाठवता ते आमच्यापर्यंत पोहोचते — त्यात तुमचा फोन नंबर किंवा खाजगी माहिती लिहू नका.

1. What Khalihan does

Khalihan shows farmers mandi (market) prices, weather, price alerts, a crop calendar and government schemes, and works out which mandi leaves the most in hand. Where Khalihan itself buys a crop, a farmer can ask Khalihan to buy theirs ("Sell to Khalihan"). It has no user accounts, no login and no advertising.

2. Information that stays on your phone

Your language, state and district, the name you type (optional), the crops you track, your price targets and alert settings, your saved mandis, the sowing dates you add to the crop calendar, and the numbers you type in "Where to sell?" and "Sell now or wait?" (quantity, vehicle rent, the town you start from, storage rent, interest) are stored only on your device. Prices are read aloud by your phone's own voice engine. Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your information, show ads, make sales calls or send marketing SMS, collect your contacts, photos or files, or track your location in the background. We do not collect your phone number — except the number you type yourself in a sell request.

6. Where the prices come from

Prices come from Agmarknet (via data.gov.in, Government of India) and the Maharashtra State Agricultural Marketing Board (MSAMB). They are shown for information only and can be 1–2 days old; always confirm at the mandi before selling. Khalihan is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any government body.

7. Your choices

You can turn off location or notifications at any time in your phone's settings; the app keeps working with the district you chose.

Khalihan has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign out of or close. To remove your data:

  1. Data on your phone (name, crops, price targets, settings): uninstall Khalihan, or open Android Settings → Apps → Khalihan → Storage → Clear data. This removes it at once and also resets the app's random IDs.
  2. Suggestions you sent: email kamranshah501@gmail.com with the subject "Khalihan – Delete my data", and tell us roughly what you wrote, when, and your district so we can find it. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.
  3. Sell requests you sent: email us the same way with the mobile number you used. We delete the request (and our WhatsApp chat copy) within 30 days and reply to confirm. If a purchase was made, we keep only what the law asks of us for accounts and tax.
  4. Usage and crash data: email us the same way and we will ask Google Firebase to delete what is linked to your phone where we can identify it. In any case this data is not linked to your name or phone number and is deleted automatically: crash reports after 90 days, usage data after 14 months at most.

What we keep: daily market price averages (they contain nothing about you) and anonymous totals such as the number of farmers using a feature.
