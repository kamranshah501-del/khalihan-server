"सूचना द्या" मध्ये तुम्ही जे लिहून पाठवता ते आमच्यापर्यंत पोहोचते — त्यात तुमचा फोन नंबर किंवा खाजगी माहिती लिहू नका.

1. What Khalihan does

Khalihan shows farmers mandi (market) prices, weather, price alerts, a crop calendar and government schemes, and works out which mandi leaves the most in hand. Where Khalihan itself buys a crop, a farmer can ask Khalihan to buy theirs ("Sell to Khalihan"); where a Khalihan Kendra shop is open, a farmer can reserve seeds, fertiliser and other farm supplies to collect there. Some of these services are open only in some places and switched on step by step. Khalihan works fully without an account; logging in with a mobile number is optional and only keeps your own settings safe in your account. It has no advertising.

2. Information that stays on your phone

Your language, state and district, the name you type (optional), the crops you track, your price targets and alert settings, your saved mandis, the sowing dates you add to the crop calendar, and the numbers you type in "Where to sell?" and "Sell now or wait?" (quantity, vehicle rent, the town you start from, storage rent, interest), your app colour choice and what the home-screen widget shows are stored only on your device — unless you log in (see "Your account backup" below). Sunrise and sunset for the automatic dark mode are worked out on the phone itself. Prices are read aloud by your phone's own voice engine. Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your information, show ads, make sales calls or send marketing SMS, collect your contacts, photos or files, or track your location in the background. We do not collect your phone number — except when you log in, or type it yourself in a sell request or a Kendra reservation.

6. Where the prices come from

Prices come from Agmarknet (via data.gov.in, Government of India) and the Maharashtra State Agricultural Marketing Board (MSAMB). They are shown for information only and can be 1–2 days old; always confirm at the mandi before selling. Khalihan is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any government body.

7. Your choices

You can turn off location or notifications at any time in your phone's settings; the app keeps working with the district you chose. You can use every price, weather, calendar and scheme feature without logging in, and log out at any time (Profile → My Account → Log Out).

In any case this data is not linked to your name or phone number and is deleted automatically: crash reports after 90 days, usage data after 14 months at most.
