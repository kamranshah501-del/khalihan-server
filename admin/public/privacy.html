1. What Khalihan does

Khalihan shows farmers mandi (market) prices, weather, price alerts, a crop calendar and government schemes, and works out which mandi leaves the most in hand. Where Khalihan itself buys a crop, a farmer can ask Khalihan to buy theirs ("Sell to Khalihan"); where a Khalihan Kendra shop is open, a farmer can reserve seeds, fertiliser and other farm supplies to collect there. Some of these services are open only in some places and switched on step by step. Khalihan works fully without an account; logging in with a mobile number is optional and only keeps your own settings safe in your account. There are no ads inside the app.

2. Information that stays on your phone

Your language, state and district, the name you type (optional), the crops you track, your price targets and alert settings, your saved mandis, the sowing dates you add to the crop calendar, and the numbers you type in "Where to sell?" and "Sell now or wait?" (quantity, vehicle rent, the town you start from, storage rent, interest), your app colour choice, the profile photo you pick, the place you save as your farm for its weather ("यहीं मेरा खेत है") and what the home-screen widget shows are stored only on your device — unless you log in (see "Your account backup" below). Sunrise and sunset for the automatic dark mode are worked out on the phone itself. Prices are read aloud by your phone's own voice engine. Searching by voice uses your phone's own voice typing (your phone's settings apply to it): Khalihan gets only the words it heard, never your voice, and does not ask for the microphone. Khalihan AI's records — your questions and its answers ("My chats", with a small copy of a photo you sent), the government forms you started or submitted with it ("Applied forms": the form's name, its application number and status lines), the papers you keep ("My documents") and the sound of its spoken answers — are kept on your phone too. "My form details" (app 1.0.9 and newer) — what you answered on a government form (for example your father's name, mobile, date of birth, address, survey number) and the user name you typed on a government site, so the next form fills itself — is kept only on your phone; you can change or delete each one in the app. Aadhaar and PAN, only if you choose to keep them there, are kept only in your phone's encrypted storage and are typed into a government site by the phone after you say yes; they are never sent to Khalihan AI, to Google or to us. Bank account numbers, OTPs and passwords are never kept. Uninstalling the app removes all of this (unless you are logged in and Khalihan AI's account backup is on — see below).

4. What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your information, show ads in the app, use your information to show you personalised ads anywhere, make sales calls or send marketing SMS, collect your contacts or your voice, or track your location in the background. We do not take photos or files from your phone — only a photo you choose to send to Khalihan AI, and the papers you choose to keep in your account. Khalihan AI's answers are made by a computer and can be wrong: it never names or measures a pesticide or fertiliser on its own (those come only from a list checked by agriculture experts), and you should check before you act. We do not collect your phone number — except when you log in, or type it yourself in a sell request or a Kendra reservation.

We advertise Khalihan itself, for example on Google. Google Ads then counts, without your name or number, how many people who saw our ad installed and opened the app.

6. Where the prices come from

Prices come from Agmarknet (via data.gov.in, Government of India) and the Maharashtra State Agricultural Marketing Board (MSAMB). They are shown for information only and can be 1–2 days old; always confirm at the mandi before selling. Khalihan is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any government body.

7. Your choices

You can turn off location or notifications at any time in your phone's settings; the app keeps working with the district you chose. You can use every price, weather, calendar and scheme feature without logging in, and log out at any time (Profile → My Account → Log Out).
